Who we are

A Power House in the Heart of Aberdeen

Omega Fire Ministries Aberdeen (Power House) Scotland is a spirit-filled family advancing the Christian faith in the Granite City.

"For our God is a consuming fire." Hebrews 12:29

Apostle Prof. Johnson Suleman, President, OFM Worldwide

Our Story

From Auchi to Aberdeen: one fire, one family.

Omega Fire Ministries International was founded by Apostle Prof. Johnson Suleman with a divine mandate of restoration, raising a people of fire, faith and excellence across the nations. Today the ministry spans continents, with branches carrying the same flame into cities around the world.

The Aberdeen branch, affectionately called the Power House, is home to a warm, multicultural family of believers in the north-east of Scotland. From our sanctuary at 360 Clifton Road we worship, serve our community and raise disciples who burn for God.

We are led locally by Min. Hope Ogom, under the covering of our General Overseer, Apostle Prof. Johnson Suleman and Rev. Dr. Lizzy Johnson Suleman.

A Registered Scottish Charity

Omega Fire Ministries Aberdeen is registered with the Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator (OSCR), Charity No. SC047125. Our charitable purpose is the advancement of religion and the provision of facilities for public worship.
