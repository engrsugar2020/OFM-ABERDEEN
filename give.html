Offering in person

Bring Your Offering to the House

Offerings are received with thanksgiving at every service — every Sunday at 10:00 AM at 360 Clifton Road. Offering envelopes are available from the ushers if you wish to give your tithe, seed or special offering discreetly.

If you are a UK taxpayer, ask about Gift Aid — it can increase the value of your giving by 25p for every £1, at no extra cost to you.
