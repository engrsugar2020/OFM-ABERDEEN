Welcome to the Power House

A church where every life finds restoration.

We are the Aberdeen expression of Omega Fire Ministries Worldwide, founded by Apostle Prof. Johnson Suleman. As a registered Scottish charity, our purpose is to advance the Christian faith and provide a place of worship, prayer and community for everyone in Aberdeen and beyond.

Led locally by Min. Hope Ogom, we gather every week to worship passionately, study the Word deeply and pray until something happens. Whatever your story, there is a seat and a family waiting for you.
