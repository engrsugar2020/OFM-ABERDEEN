Weekly Activities

There Is a Place for You Every Week

From Sunday celebration to midnight fire, join the Power House family in worship, word and prayer.

"Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together..." Hebrews 10:25
Every Sunday

Sunday Service

10:00 AM to 11:00 AM 360 Clifton Road, Aberdeen

Our main worship gathering, with passionate praise, the undiluted Word of God and a family atmosphere for all ages.

1st Sunday of the month

Anointing Service

8:30 AM 360 Clifton Road, Aberdeen

Begin the new month under the anointing with a special early service of prayer, impartation and blessing before Sunday Service.

Every Tuesday

Bible Study

8:15 PM to 9:15 PM

A deep, practical journey through the Scriptures. Bring your Bible, your questions and a hunger for the Word.

Every Friday

Solution Hour

10:00 PM

An hour of prayer and the Word for every situation. Bring your needs before God. Held every Friday except the last Friday of the month.

Last Friday of the month

Fire Night

10:00 PM to 1:00 AM

A night of fire, with worship, deliverance and miracles, joining Fire & Miracle Night with Apostle Johnson Suleman.

Can't make it in person?

Follow OFM Aberdeen on Facebook, YouTube and TikTok for live streams, messages and announcements.

Your seat is already waiting.

We meet at 360 Clifton Road, Aberdeen AB24 4DX, Scotland. Arrive a few minutes early and our welcome team will look after you like family. Children are fully welcome, and so are you, exactly as you are.
